Wholesale Program
For congregations & gift shops

A piece of the atelier,
at your community's door.

We collaborate with synagogues, Jewish gift shops, JCCs, and Hillels nationwide — bringing our heirloom Judaica into the spaces where Jewish life is gathered. Custom collections, congregation-specific commissions, and standing wholesale relationships available.

  • 01 Gift shops Curated wholesale assortments across all three collections, refreshed for each season and holiday.
  • 02 Synagogues B'nai mitzvah gift programs, congregational mezuzot, and commissioned pieces for sanctuaries.
  • 03 JCCs & Hillels Community-scale orders, event partnerships, and bespoke pieces for capital campaigns and dedications.
  • 04 Custom commissions One-of-a-kind pieces marking installations, anniversaries, or honored members of your community.
